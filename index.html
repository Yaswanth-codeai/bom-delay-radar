        Transparent by design

        How it was built

        Data and target

        22,886 flights departing Mumbai in 2024, with schedule, route, airline, weather and airport-traffic fields. A flight counts as delayed when it leaves more than 15 minutes after its scheduled time.

        Columns that describe what happened after departure (actual times and delay minutes) are never used as inputs, because that would leak the answer.

        Honest testing

        Training used January – September (17,132 flights). Testing used October – December (5,754 flights). Shuffling flights randomly would flatter the score, so the split follows the calendar.

        Two models

        The full report uses a stacked ensemble of logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting. This page runs a compact gradient-boosted model (13 inputs, 120 small trees) exported to JavaScript so it works offline, and it scores about the same on the same test months.

        Limits to keep in mind

        Hourly airport traffic does most of the work. Ideas such as “delays ripple through the schedule” added nothing measurable here (see the ablation above). Predictions are for learning and demonstration, not for deciding whether to travel.
